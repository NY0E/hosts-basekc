How it works

Five steps, start to live URL

No GitHub. No terminal. No monthly subscription.

Which AI do you need?  This prompt is built and tested for Claude — you'll need a free account at claude.ai (no credit card required). It will also work in ChatGPT and Google Gemini with similar results. Avoid Perplexity for this — it's a research tool, not a code builder. Whichever you use, skip the "Try without signing up" option; the output is too long and will get cut off before it finishes.

1

Copy the prompt below

Hit the "Copy prompt" button — the whole thing goes to your clipboard, variables and all.

2

Fill in your property details

Paste into any text editor, fill in the variables at the top. Your name, neighborhood, WiFi, local spots. Takes about 5 minutes.

Tip: right-click your address in Google Maps to grab your coordinates for the weather widget
3

Paste into Claude and send

Go to claude.ai, start a new conversation, paste the whole thing, and hit Send. Your guidebook builds in about 60 seconds.

4

Save the HTML file

Copy Claude's output into Notepad or TextEdit and save as index.html.

Mac users: Format > Make Plain Text in TextEdit before pasting
5

Drag onto Netlify Drop

Go to app.netlify.com/drop, drag your file onto the page, and you have a live public URL in under 60 seconds. Free forever.

Want guide.yourproperty.com? Create a free Netlify account and connect your domain — about 10 minutes
